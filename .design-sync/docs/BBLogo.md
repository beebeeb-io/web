The beebeeb.io wordmark as text: "beebeeb" in the ink colour with an amber ".io". `size` is the font size in px (the app chrome uses 14); `light` renders it white for dark surfaces. Products other than Drive (Pass, VPN, Mail, Chat) keep this wordmark and add their product name next to it; they never recolour it.

```tsx
<BBLogo size={14} />
```
